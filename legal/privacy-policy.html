[APP_NAME] ("the app", "we") is a tool operated by [YOUR_NAME_OR_ENTITY] that lets the account owner publish their own videos to their own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Information we handle

  • TikTok authorization tokens (OAuth access token and refresh token) that you obtain after authorizing the app with your own TikTok account.
  • Basic creator information returned by TikTok's API (such as your nickname and allowed privacy settings) used to prepare a post.

2. How we use it

The information is used only to authenticate with TikTok and to publish the content you choose, on your behalf, to your own account. It is not used for any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

The app runs locally on your own device. Tokens and settings are stored on that device (for example in a local configuration file) and are not transmitted to any server operated by us. Keep your device and configuration file secure.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Data is exchanged only directly between your device and TikTok's official API.

5. Retention and revoking access

You may delete your local tokens at any time, and you can revoke the app's access from your TikTok account settings (Security & permissions → Manage app permissions). Once revoked, the tokens can no longer be used.

6. Children

The app is not directed to anyone under the age required by TikTok's own terms.
